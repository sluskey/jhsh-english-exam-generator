| 項目 | 錦和高中規範標準 | 備註說明 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **頁面大小** | **A4** (21.0 cm × 29.7 cm) | 試務組將等比等寬印製至 B4 雙面考卷 |

## 三、 選項智慧橫排排版規則（省紙規範）

為有效控制考卷頁數並維持試卷視覺簡潔，選擇題選項依據選項長度實施智慧排版：

1. **四選一列橫排（長度 $\le 12$ 字元）**：
   ```text
   1. Look! The children are playing happily in the ________.
      (A) park　　(B) river　　(C) bank　　(D) museum
   ```
2. **兩選一列（2×2 排列，長度 $13 \sim 25$ 字元）**：
   ```text
   2. Tina was very hungry, so she ate two large bowls of noodles.
      (A) went to bed early          (B) drank a lot of water
      (C) ate two bowls of noodles    (D) cooked dinner for us
   ```
3. **單列單選排列（長度 $> 25$ 字元或附圖時）**：
   ```text
   3. Which of the following is TRUE about the festival?
      (A) It takes place on the second Sunday of May every year.
      (B) People usually give red envelopes to their family members.
      (C) It was celebrated for the first time in England in 1914.
      (D) Children need to clean their bedrooms before the event starts.
   ```
